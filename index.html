01 — THE STORY

Operation Torch

Operation Torch was the Allied invasion of French North Africa during the Second World War.

The operation began on 8 November 1942 and involved American and British forces conducting amphibious landings at Casablanca, Oran and Algiers.

The operation was designed to establish an Allied presence in North Africa and create pressure on Axis forces operating in the Mediterranean region.

The campaign also demonstrated the growing ability of American forces to conduct a large overseas amphibious operation alongside British forces.
