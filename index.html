Operation Torch

Operation Torch was the Allied invasion of French North Africa during the Second World War. The operation began on 8 November 1942 and involved American and British forces.

The landings took place at Casablanca, Oran and Algiers, opening a major Allied campaign in North Africa.

Relative Strength

Allied Forces

American and British forces conducted amphibious landings supported by naval and air power.

Vichy French Forces

French forces in North Africa initially opposed the Allied landings before the fighting ended.

Operational Plans

Allied Plan

The Allies planned simultaneous landings at Casablanca, Oran and Algiers.

French Defence

Vichy French forces prepared to defend the strategically important ports and coastal areas.

Timeline

8 November 1942

Allied landings began in French North Africa.

9–10 November

Fighting continued around the landing areas.

11 November 1942

Fighting ended following agreements between Allied and French authorities.
